**Quick gist:** The **22nd Africa Movie Academy Awards** took place on **Sunday 15 November 2026** at the Marriott Hotel, Ikeja, Lagos. Nigeria won **eight awards**. **Wale Ojo** won Best Actor in a Leading Role for *Lights Out*, and **Linda Ejiofor** won Best Actress in a Supporting Role for *The Herd*. The Kenya–Nigeria co-production *One Woman, One Bra* took **Best Film**.

Nollywood's finest dressed up, showed up and took home some serious hardware. Here's what you need to know.

## The big winners

- **Best Film:** *One Woman, One Bra* (Kenya/Nigeria). It also won Best Debut Feature Film.
- **Best Actor in a Leading Role:** Wale Ojo, *Lights Out* (Nigeria)
- **Best Actress in a Leading Role:** Naky Sy Savane, *The Color of Exile* (Côte d'Ivoire)
- **Best Actress in a Supporting Role:** Linda Ejiofor, *The Herd* (Nigeria)

*The Color of Exile* from Côte d'Ivoire led the night with four trophies, and Kenyan cinema also had a big showing. It was a reminder that the African film scene is much bigger than Nollywood alone.

## The Herd keeps winning

Daniel Etim-Effiong's ***The Herd*** was one of Nigeria's standout films of the night. Beyond Linda Ejiofor's acting win, it also took **Achievement in Production Design** and **Achievement in Make-up**. Etim-Effiong was also nominated for Best Actor.

## Linda Ejiofor's incredible year

This caps a huge 2026 for Linda Ejiofor. Back in May, she became the first actress to win **both** Best Lead and Best Supporting Actress at the AMVCAs on the same night. Adding an AMAA to that makes her one of the most decorated Nollywood actresses of the year.

## Wale Ojo: still got it

Wale Ojo has been a Nollywood heavyweight for years, and *Lights Out* has now earned him one of the continent's top acting awards. He was also nominated at the AMVCAs in May for *3 Cold Dishes*, so it's been a strong year for him too.

## Where to watch

Many AMAA winners do a festival run before landing on streaming. Keep an eye on Netflix, Prime Video and the newer African platforms over the coming months for *The Herd*, *Lights Out* and *One Woman, One Bra*.

Whether you're home or watching from abroad, here's the guide.

## In Nigerian cinemas

**Loving Itohan** (from 6 November, FilmOne)
Nancy Isime and Uzor Arukwe lead a romantic drama about Itohan, her childhood love and the experiences that shape her. Expect big chemistry and big emotions.

**Dear Ajayi** (wide release from 13 November, FilmOne)
Set in 1990s south-west Nigeria, two estranged sisters are forced to live together again to care for their paralysed mother. With **Bimbo Akintola** and **Tosin Adeyemi**. It had a limited run from September and is now everywhere.

**Issakaba: The Return** (13 November, Blue Pictures)
Yes, *that* Issakaba. Twenty-five years after the original vigilante classic, the story returns in present-day Nigeria with **Sam Dede**, **Chidi Mokeme**, **Iyabo Ojo**, **Regina Daniels** and **Nosa Rex**. Nostalgia is guaranteed.

**Five Figurines** (13 November, Cinemax)
A heavyweight cast: **Bimbo Akintola**, **Ini Edo**, **Odunlade Adekola**, **Ireti Doyle** and **Uche Montana**. Plot details are being kept quiet.

**Ray of Hope** (20 November, Cinemax)
A drama set in the fashion world, with **Jide Kene Achufusi**, **Stan Nze**, **Bucci Franklin**, **Uzor Arukwe**, **Zack Orji** and **Sunshine Rosman**.

## On Netflix (watch anywhere)

**The Covenant, season 2**
The series is back, with a new storyline set in **South Africa**. Season 1 starred Gideon Okeke, Zubby Michael, Uzee Usman and Sola Sobowale. Reports gave slightly different release dates in early November, so search for it on Netflix in your region.

## Watching from abroad?

Most cinema releases make their way to streaming within a few months. If you're in the UK, some Nollywood titles also get screenings at diaspora film nights and selected cinemas, so follow the films' social pages for UK dates.

## Our pick

If you only watch one: **Dear Ajayi** for the drama, or **Issakaba: The Return** if you grew up on the original and want to see how it's aged.

**Quick gist:** After beating Tanzania **3-1 in Uyo**, the Super Eagles were held **0-0 in Zanzibar**. Nigeria have **7 points** from four games. Group leaders **Guinea-Bissau** then beat Madagascar 2-0 to move on to **10 points**. Because Tanzania qualify automatically as co-hosts, **only one more team from Group L** goes to AFCON 2027, so it's effectively Nigeria vs Guinea-Bissau.

Another qualifying campaign, another set of nerves. Here's where things stand.

## How we got here

- **Matchday 1:** Nigeria 2-1 Madagascar (Uyo)
- **Matchday 2:** lost 3-0 to Guinea-Bissau. Ouch.
- **Matchday 3:** Nigeria 3-1 Tanzania (Uyo). Tolu Arokodare came off the bench and scored twice in eight minutes.
- **Matchday 4:** Tanzania 0-0 Nigeria (Zanzibar). Coach Eric Chelle made seven changes, and the reshuffled team couldn't break Tanzania down.

## Why Tanzania's result matters less than you'd think

AFCON 2027 will be hosted by **Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania** from **19 June to 17 July 2027**. As co-hosts, Tanzania already have a place. That means Group L's other qualifying spot goes to the **highest-placed team among Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau and Madagascar**.

Guinea-Bissau beat Madagascar 2-0 in the same round we drew, which puts them three points clear. That 3-0 defeat on matchday 2 could come back to haunt us, because it also gives them the edge if it comes down to head-to-head.

## What Nigeria need

- **Win the remaining games.** No more slip-ups against Madagascar.
- **Beat Guinea-Bissau in Bissau.** That return fixture is effectively a final, and pundits are already backing the Eagles to do it.
- **Hope Guinea-Bissau drop points** elsewhere.

It's no longer fully in our hands. Nigeria need wins and some help, with no room left for mistakes.

## The pressure on Chelle

Pundits, including former Super Eagles coach Jo Bonfrere, have warned that Nigeria must improve or risk missing AFCON 2027. After also missing the 2026 World Cup, missing AFCON would be a disaster for the NFF and for Chelle.

Questions remain about team selection, the goalkeeper position and why the team looks so different from one match to the next.

## Diaspora fans

If you're watching from the UK or US, most AFCON qualifiers stream through broadcasters with African football rights or official CAF channels, depending on where you are. Check listings closer to matchday.
